# Findings Template

## Experiment question
State the product decision being evaluated.

## Data quality
Document user uniqueness, missing values, assignment balance, and exclusions.

## Primary result
Report control conversion, treatment conversion, absolute lift, relative lift, confidence interval, and p-value after running the analysis.

## Segment checks
Compare pre-specified segments such as device or traffic source. Avoid presenting post-hoc segment differences as definitive without sufficient sample size.

## Business interpretation
Separate statistical evidence from practical impact and document the decision rule used by the experiment owner.
